Frequently Asked Questions

What qualifies as a dental emergency?

Severe tooth pain, persistent bleeding, a knocked-out tooth, abscesses or facial swelling, and cracked or broken teeth requiring immediate attention are all dental emergencies.

How fast can I see an Emergency Dentist in Orlando?

We prioritize urgent cases and aim to get you seen on the exact same day or within hours of your call through our 24/7 line.

When is an Emergency Tooth Extraction necessary?

An extraction is typically necessary when a tooth is severely decayed, fractured beyond repair, or causing dangerous infections that risk spreading.

Should I go to the hospital ER or an ER Dentistry clinic?

Hospital ERs usually manage trauma and pain but cannot perform dental repairs. An emergency dental clinic delivers actual restorative procedures, extractions, and specialized treatments.

What should I do if my tooth gets completely knocked out?

Keep the tooth moist in milk or saline, avoid touching the root, and call our emergency hotline immediately—reimplantation is most successful within 60 minutes.
